I have been battling mental illness for most of my life. I currently am diagnosed as bipolar1. I do my best to continue to create artwork that is original and interesting. My inspiration are my two sons and my mother who has been there to support me through difficult times dealing with mental illness, drug addiction and mania. I thank God for the talent that was given to me and the art that I have been able to produce in my lifetime.
